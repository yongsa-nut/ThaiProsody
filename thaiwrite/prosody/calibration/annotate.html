Rubric (mirrors the judge prompt)

The two meaning axes are the main task. Read the topic (top-left), then the poem, then rate.

Topicality — does it address the topic?

  • 1 off-topic · 3 topic only mentioned · 5 substantively about it

Coherence — are the Thai lines intelligible & connected?

  • 1 word salad · 3 locally grammatical but disjointed · 5 fully connected

Hard constraints

  • Do not judge syllable count / tone / rhyme in the meaning axes — that's the optional meter row.
  • Do not penalise rare or archaic vocabulary (classical Thai is lexically rich).

Meter dimensions (optional) — ✓ compliant / ✗ violated

  • count = syllables per วรรค match · tone (เอก/โท) = required tone marks at bound positions
  • rhyme (สัมผัส) = required rhyme links hold · weight (ครุ/ลหุ) = heavy/light pattern matches (ฉันท์)
